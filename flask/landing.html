The problem

Written faster than it's read

Code gets written faster than anyone can read it. The mistakes that matter are architectural, and they surface months later.

Asking loses the big picture

Asking an assistant “how does this work?” answers one question at a time, and the shape of the whole system never comes into view.

Explore instead

Start broad, pinpoint a part, go deeper and deeper, down to the line, and always be able to trace your way back.
